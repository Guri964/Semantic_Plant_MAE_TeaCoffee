Process

How LeafScan works

Four parallel AI systems analyze your leaf image and combine results through a knowledge base to deliver a complete diagnosis.

Upload leaf

Take a clear photo of the leaf from your plantation and upload it.

AI analysis

Crop model, health model, and color filter run simultaneously on the image.

KB matching

Results matched against a manually-researched disease knowledge base.

Get diagnosis

Disease name, severity, description, and AI-generated treatment recommendation.

Capabilities

Built for plantation farmers

Automatic crop detection

No dropdown needed. The system identifies tea or coffee from the leaf image automatically with 97.90% accuracy.

Health classification

Hybrid EfficientNet-B4 + Vision Transformer model detects healthy vs unhealthy leaves with 95.50% accuracy.

HSV color analysis

OpenCV rule-based color filter detects brown, black, yellow, white, and gray disease signatures in the affected region.

Severity assessment

Calculates diseased-area pixels divided by total leaf pixels and classifies as Mild, Moderate, or Severe.

Knowledge base matching

Weighted similarity search across crop, health status, and colors returns the best-matching disease from a researched SQLite database.

AI recommendation

LLM generates a practical, farmer-friendly treatment recommendation based on the diagnosed condition and severity level.

About

Built on real plantation research

LeafScan uses a semantic search-based approach to plant disease classification. Instead of one model predicting disease names directly, it breaks the problem into three signals — crop identification, health classification, and color detection — and combines them through a hand-curated knowledge base built from real agricultural disease research.

Trained on 3,327 real field-captured images from tea and coffee plantations. The knowledge base can be extended with new diseases without retraining any model.
