How the pipeline works

1

Crop identification

EfficientNet-B4 (trained from scratch on 3,325 images) predicts whether the leaf is tea or coffee. Test accuracy: 97.90%.

2

Health classification

Hybrid EfficientNet-B4 + ViT-B/16 predicts healthy or unhealthy. Test accuracy: 95.50%.

3

Color filter + severity

OpenCV HSV color filtering detects disease colors. Severity = diseased pixels ÷ leaf pixels × 100.

4

Knowledge base matching

Weighted similarity — crop (50%), health (30%), color (20%) — returns the best-matching disease from a manually-researched SQLite database of 16 conditions.

5

AI recommendation

Groq LLM generates a practical, farmer-friendly treatment recommendation based on the diagnosed condition and severity.
